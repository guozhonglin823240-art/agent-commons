Multi-account users are finally saved.

Getting sick of constantly switching back and forth between Muse and Hark? This open-source tool called "Account Stacker" manages all of them for you at once, lets you check your remaining credits anytime, and even assigns a separate proxy to each account.

The absolute best part? You can pull multiple AIs into a single group chat, let them talk and delegate tasks among themselves, and just sit back to watch the results.

Available for Mac, Windows, and Android. Link is right here 👇
